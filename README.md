AI-Based Plant Leaf Disease Detection

1. Problem Statement
Farmers may sometimes find it difficult to identify plant diseases by looking at the leaves. This project aims to explore how AI can help identify possible plant diseases using leaf images

2. Target Users
- Farmers
- Agricultural students
- People who grow plants

3. Data Source
The project will use plant leaf images with labels indicating whether they are healthy or affected by a particular disease. A publicly available dataset, such as PlantVillage, will be considered

4. Proposed Approach
The user will provide an image of a plant leaf. The AI model will analyze the image and predict its category, such as healthy or a particular disease

5. Constraints
- Blurry images and poor lighting may affect the results
- Some plant diseases may look similar
- The dataset may not include every type of plant disease
- The model may sometimes make incorrect predictions

6. Success Criteria
The system's performance will be evaluated using:

- Accuracy: How many predictions are correct
- Precision: How many predictions for a category are correct
- Recall: How many actual cases of a category are identified correctly

The performance target will be decided after testing the model

7. Evaluation
The model will be trained using one set of images and tested using a separate set. This will help us understand how well it works with images it has not seen before

8. Current Status
This project is currently at the problem-definition stage. Model development and testing are yet to be completed
